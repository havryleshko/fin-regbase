# OFAC — Licensing, SDN Delisting, and Unblocking Procedures

## Overview

31 CFR Part 501 Subpart E establishes the procedures for obtaining OFAC licences, seeking reconsideration of SDN List designations, and requesting release of property blocked due to mistaken identity or typographical error. These procedures apply across all OFAC sanctions programmes.

---

## Licensing — §501.801

OFAC administers two types of licences. Both authorise transactions that would otherwise be prohibited under an applicable sanctions programme.

### General Licences — §501.801(a)

**General licences** authorise entire **categories** of transactions under specified conditions. They are:
- **Self-executing** — no application to OFAC required; a person who meets all the conditions of a general licence may proceed with the transaction without contacting OFAC
- **Programme-specific** — published in the regulations applicable to each particular sanctions programme (the relevant Part of 31 CFR Chapter V) and/or on OFAC's website at *ofac.treasury.gov*
- Available to all persons who qualify under the stated conditions

**Compliance risk:** A general licence is only available if all stated conditions are met. Relying on a general licence without verifying that every condition is satisfied is a common SCP breakdown (Root Cause 5: misinterpretation or over-reliance on licensing requirements). **(§501.801(a); Framework p. 9)**

### Specific Licences — §501.801(b)

**Specific licences** authorise a **named person** to engage in a **specific transaction** or category of transactions that is not covered by a general licence. Key features:

- **Application required:** Submitted to OFAC's Licensing Division via the OFAC License Application Page at *licensing.ofac.treas.gov* or using Form TD-F 90-22.54
- **Information required:** The applicant must supply all information specified by OFAC, including the names and interests of all parties with involvement in or interest in the proposed transaction; all parties' interests must be disclosed, including agents
- **Processing:** Only one copy of each application should be submitted; multiple copies may delay processing
- **Verbal presentation:** In rare circumstances, applicants may request an oral presentation by submitting a request via the OFAC License Application Page; such requests are rarely granted
- **Denial and reapplication:** Denial of a specific licence does not preclude the filing of a further application at any time on the basis of new facts or changed circumstances **(§501.801(b)(5))**
- **OFAC decision = final agency action:** The decision of OFAC acting on behalf of the Secretary of the Treasury with respect to a specific licence application constitutes **final agency action** **(§501.802)**
- **FOIA protection:** Information submitted to OFAC pursuant to §501.801 is protected from FOIA disclosure where OFAC reasonably foresees disclosure would harm a FOIA-exempt interest **(§501.801(b)(6))**

### Reports Under Specific Licences — §501.801(b)(4)

As a condition of issuance, a specific licence may require the licensee to file reports relating to the transactions authorised by the licence. Reports must be sent in accordance with the instructions in the specific licence.

### Rulemaking and Amendments — §§501.803–501.804

OFAC may amend, modify, or revoke any licence, ruling, authorisation, instruction, or order at any time. **(§501.803)**

OFAC rulemaking is **exempt from notice-and-comment requirements** under the Administrative Procedure Act (5 USC 553) because it involves foreign affairs functions of the United States. **(§501.804(a))**

## Unblocking Property Blocked Due to Mistaken Identity or Typographical Error — §501.806

Where property has been blocked by a US person pursuant to OFAC regulations, but the blocking was the result of **mistaken identity** (e.g., a name match on a different person) or a **typographical or similar error**, the affected party may seek release through the following procedure:

### Procedure

1. Submit a request to **OFACReport@treasury.gov** with the phrase **"31 CFR 501.806 — Request for a Compliance Release"** in the subject line. **(§501.806(b))**

2. The request must include:
   - Name, address, telephone number, and email address of the person seeking release **(§501.806(c))**
   - Name of the person who holds or filed the initial blocked property report **(§501.806(d)(1))**
   - Actual or estimated value of the blocked property **(§501.806(d)(2))**
   - Date of the blocking included in the initial report **(§501.806(d)(3))**
   - Copy of a valid government-issued identification document, SSN or EIN for the person believed to have been blocked in error **(§501.806(d)(4))**
   - The **ORS identification number** from the initial blocked property report **(§501.806(d)(5))**
   - Description of the property or underlying transaction **(§501.806(d)(6))**
   - Narrative explaining why the property was blocked in error **(§501.806(d)(7))**

3. OFAC may request additional material from the applicant pursuant to §501.602. **(§501.806(e))**

4. OFAC reviews all submissions and determines whether the property should be released. If OFAC determines the property should be released, it directs the person holding the blocked property to release it to the appropriate party. **(§501.806(f))**

## SDN List Delisting and Reconsideration — §501.807

A **sanctioned person** (or the owner of property blocked or otherwise subject to OFAC sanctions) may petition OFAC for removal from the SDN List or any other OFAC-maintained list of sanctioned persons or property.

### Grounds for Petition

A person may submit arguments and evidence establishing that:
- Insufficient basis exists for the sanctions
- The circumstances resulting in the sanctions no longer apply
- The person proposes remedial steps (e.g., corporate reorganisation, resignation from positions in a blocked entity) that the person believes would negate the basis for sanctions

A person owning a majority interest in blocked property (e.g., a vessel) may propose the sale of the property, with proceeds placed in a blocked interest-bearing account less costs incurred during blocking. **(§501.807(a))**

### Procedure

1. Submit the petition to **OFAC.Reconsideration@treasury.gov**. **(§501.807(a))**

2. OFAC will **review all information and arguments** submitted, may request clarifying, corroborating, or additional information, and may (at its discretion) agree to a meeting — though meetings are not required and OFAC may decline. **(§501.807(b)(1)–(2))**

3. After completing its review, OFAC will **provide a written decision** to the person seeking removal. **(§501.807(b)(3))**

## Public Availability of Information — §501.805

OFAC maintains public availability of records consistent with FOIA (5 USC 552) and the Privacy Act (5 USC 552a). Programme-specific forms are available on OFAC's website at *ofac.treasury.gov*.

**Civil penalty public disclosure:** After civil penalty proceedings or informal settlements conclude, OFAC makes the following information publicly available at least monthly for entity proceedings:
- Name and address of the entity
- Sanctions programme involved
- Brief description of the violation or alleged violation
- Whether the proceeding resulted in an informal settlement or formal penalty
- Whether the entity voluntarily self-disclosed
- Amount of penalty or agreed settlement

For proceedings involving **individuals**, only aggregate statistics are released — individual names are not publicly disclosed. **(§501.805(d))**

## Comparison: General Licence vs. Specific Licence vs. Delisting

| Feature | General Licence | Specific Licence | SDN Delisting |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|--------------|
| Requires OFAC application? | No — self-executing | Yes | Yes |
| Covers | Category of transactions | Named person / specific transaction | Removal from sanctions list entirely |
| OFAC decision = final agency action? | N/A | Yes (§501.802) | Written decision |
| Can denial be reconsidered? | N/A | Yes — reapplication any time on new facts | Yes — further petition permitted |
| FOIA protection for submitted info? | N/A | Yes (§501.801(b)(6)) | Yes |

## Key Points for Agents

- **General licences are self-executing — but only when every condition is satisfied.** A common SCP failure (Root Cause 5) is over-reliance on a general licence without verifying that all conditions are met. If the transaction does not fit squarely within a general licence's conditions, a specific licence application is required. Proceeding without a valid licence while believing a general licence applies is an apparent violation. **(§501.801(a); Framework p. 9)**
- **Denial of a specific licence does not preclude reapplication.** OFAC may deny a licence application and the applicant may reapply at any time on the basis of new facts or changed circumstances. Licence denial is not res judicata. OFAC's decision on an application is final agency action for APA purposes, but reapplication on new facts is always available. **(§501.801(b)(5); §501.802)**
- **Mistaken-identity unblocking requests cite "31 CFR 501.806" and go to OFACReport@treasury.gov; SDN delisting petitions go to OFAC.Reconsideration@treasury.gov.** These are distinct procedures with distinct email addresses. The ORS identification number from the original blocked property report is required for §501.806 requests — institutions must retain their blocking reports and ORS reference numbers. **(§§501.806, 501.807)**
- **OFAC rulemaking is exempt from APA notice-and-comment.** OFAC may issue, amend, or revoke any licence, rule, or authorisation at any time without prior public comment because its actions involve US foreign affairs functions. This means sanctions programme changes (including new designations and new general licences) can take effect immediately without a transition period. **(§§501.803–501.804)**
