# Regulation E — Remittance Transfers (12 CFR Part 1005, Subpart B)

## Overview

Subpart B (§§1005.30–1005.36) imposes a distinct regulatory framework on international consumer money transfers — covering pre-payment disclosures, receipts, error resolution, cancellation rights, and rules for scheduled transfers. These requirements are separate from, and in addition to, the general Reg E requirements in Subpart A.

Subpart B was added in 2012 and implemented the Dodd-Frank Act's remittance transfer provisions. It applies to any person that provides international money transfers to consumers in the normal course of business — including banks, money service businesses, and payment fintechs. **(§§1005.30–1005.36)**

---

## Definitions (§1005.30)

**Remittance transfer:** The electronic transfer of funds from a **sender** (a consumer in a US state) to a **designated recipient** in a foreign country, requested by the sender from a **remittance transfer provider**. **(§1005.30(e)(1))**

Applies regardless of whether:
- The sender holds an account with the remittance transfer provider.
- The transaction is also an EFT under Subpart A.

**Exclusions from "remittance transfer":**
- **Small-value transactions:** Transfers of **$15 or less**. **(§1005.30(e)(2)(i))**
- **Securities and commodities transfers:** Transfers excluded from the definition of EFT under §1005.3(c)(4). **(§1005.30(e)(2)(ii))**

**Remittance transfer provider:** Any person that provides remittance transfers for a consumer in the normal course of its business. **(§1005.30(f)(1))**

**Normal course of business — safe harbor:** A person is deemed NOT to be providing remittance transfers in the normal course of business if the person provided **500 or fewer** remittance transfers in the previous calendar year **and** provides **500 or fewer** in the current calendar year. Both conditions must be met. **(§1005.30(f)(2)(i))**

If a person previously below the safe harbor threshold provides more than 500 transfers in the current year, the person has a **reasonable period not exceeding 6 months** to begin complying. **(§1005.30(f)(2)(ii))**

**Sender:** A consumer in a US state who requests a remittance transfer provider to send a remittance transfer primarily for personal, family, or household purposes. **(§1005.30(g))**

**Third-party fees:**
- **Covered third-party fees:** Fees imposed by persons other than the provider, except fees imposed by the designated recipient's institution for receiving funds into an account. Example: correspondent bank fees. **(§1005.30(h)(1))**
- **Non-covered third-party fees:** Fees imposed by the designated recipient's institution for receiving funds into an account (unless the institution is acting as the provider's agent). Example: receiving bank wire acceptance fees. **(§1005.30(h)(2))**

## Pre-Payment Disclosure Requirements (§1005.31(b)(1))

Before the sender pays, the remittance transfer provider must disclose:

| Item | Required term |
|------|--------------|
| Amount to be transferred (in recipient currency) | "Transfer Amount" |
| Fees imposed by the provider | "Transfer Fees" |
| Taxes collected by the provider | "Transfer Taxes" |
| Total amount paid by sender (principal + fees + taxes) | "Total" |
| Exchange rate used | "Exchange Rate" |
| Amount recipient will receive (in recipient currency), including covered third-party fees | "Transfer Amount" (recipient-side) |
| Covered third-party fees (if any) | "Other Fees" |
| Total amount recipient will receive (in recipient currency) | "Total to Recipient" |
| Non-covered third-party fee statement (optional disclosure of estimated amount) | Prescribed model language from Appendix A-30 |

**(§1005.31(b)(1)(i)–(viii))**

The exchange rate must be rounded to no fewer than **two** and no more than **four** decimal places. **(§1005.31(b)(1)(iv))**

---

## Receipt Requirements (§1005.31(b)(2))

After the sender pays, the provider must provide a receipt containing all pre-payment disclosure items plus:

1. **Date funds available** to the designated recipient (using the term "Date Available" or substantially similar)
2. **Recipient information:** Name, and telephone number/address if provided by the sender
3. **Statement about error resolution and cancellation rights** (using Model Form A-37 language or substantially similar)
4. **Provider contact information:** Name, telephone number(s), and website
5. **State agency contact information:** Name, telephone, and website of the state agency that licenses or charters the provider
6. **CFPB contact information:** Name and toll-free telephone number
7. **Transfer Date** (for transfers scheduled at least 3 business days in advance) **(§1005.31(b)(2)(vii))**

---

## Combined Disclosure Option (§1005.31(b)(3))

A provider may combine the pre-payment disclosure and receipt into a single document. If the combined disclosure is provided and the sender completes the transfer, the provider must also provide **proof of payment** when payment is made. The proof of payment must be in a retainable, clear and conspicuous written or electronic form. **(§1005.31(b)(3)(i))**

---

## Long Form Error Resolution and Cancellation Notice (§1005.31(b)(4))

Upon request, the provider must promptly provide the sender a notice describing the sender's error resolution and cancellation rights. Model Form A-36 provides the prescribed long-form language. **(§1005.31(b)(4))**

---

## Format Requirements (§1005.31(c))

**Grouping:** The transfer amount, fees, taxes, and total (items (b)(1)(i)–(iii)) must be grouped together. The exchange rate, recipient amounts, and non-covered fee statement (items (b)(1)(iv)–(viii)) must also be grouped together. **(§1005.31(c)(1))**

**Prominence:** The pre-payment disclosure must appear on the front of the page or screen. Written disclosures must be in at least equal prominence to each other. **(§1005.31(c)(3))**

**Segregation:** For written or electronic disclosures (other than mobile/text), the required disclosures must be segregated from all other information and contain only information directly related to the required disclosures. **(§1005.31(c)(4))**

**Foreign language:** Disclosures must be made in English and, if applicable, in each foreign language the provider uses to advertise, solicit, or market remittance transfer services at the office where the transaction is conducted or asserted as an error. **(§1005.31(g)(1))**

For oral, mobile, or text message transactions, disclosures must be in the language the sender uses with the provider to conduct the transaction. **(§1005.31(g)(2))**

---

## Timing of Disclosures (§1005.31(e))

| Disclosure | Timing |
|-----------|--------|
| Pre-payment disclosure | Before the sender pays (before payment is made for the transfer) |
| Receipt | At the time of payment (or within 1 business day after date of payment for oral/telephone transactions) |
| Long form error resolution notice | Promptly upon request |

---

## Estimated Disclosures (§1005.32)

Exact amounts are required, but permanent exceptions permit estimated disclosures in specific circumstances:

**Permanent exception — recipient country determination not possible (§1005.32(b)(1)):**
Estimates permitted for exchange rate, transfer amount in recipient currency, covered third-party fees, and "total to recipient" if the laws or payment methods of the recipient country do not permit determination of the exact amounts. The provider may rely on the CFPB's published list of countries qualifying for this exception.

**Permanent exception — transfers scheduled in advance (§1005.32(b)(2)):**
For transfers scheduled **5 or more business days** before the date of transfer, estimates are permitted for exchange rate, fees, and recipient amounts — using the exchange rate the provider would use for a same-day transfer on the date of disclosure.

**Permanent exception — non-covered third-party fees (§1005.32(b)(3)):**
Estimates are always permitted for non-covered third-party fees (fees imposed by the designated recipient's institution), based on reasonable sources of information.

**Bases for estimates (§1005.32(c)):**
Exchange rate estimates must be based on one of:
- The most recent exchange rate set by the recipient country's central bank (reported by a Federal Reserve Bank) — for international ACH qualifying for the country-exception
- The most recent publicly available wholesale exchange rate, plus any applicable spread
- The most recent exchange rate offered or used by the person setting the exchange rate for the transfer

## Error Resolution (§1005.33)

### What Is an "Error"?

The following are errors under §1005.33(a)(1):

1. **Incorrect amount paid** — sender paid more than disclosed (unless disclosure used a permitted estimate and difference results from actual exchange rate/fees/taxes rather than estimated amounts)
2. **Computational or bookkeeping error** by the provider
3. **Failure to deliver the disclosed amount** to the recipient — unless: (A) disclosure used a permitted estimate and difference results from actual rates; (B) failure resulted from extraordinary circumstances outside provider's control; or (C) difference results from non-covered third-party fees and the required disclaimer was disclosed
4. **Failure to make funds available by the disclosed date** — unless: (A) extraordinary circumstances outside provider's control; (B) delays due to OFAC or BSA fraud screening requirements; (C) fraudulent intent by the sender; or (D) sender provided an incorrect account number or recipient institution identifier (see below)

The following are **NOT** errors:
- A status inquiry about a remittance transfer (unless funds not available by disclosed date)
- A request for information for tax or recordkeeping purposes
- A change requested by the designated recipient
- A change in amount or currency received because the provider relied on sender-provided information **(§1005.33(a)(2))**

### Notice of Error — Sender's Window

A sender has **180 days** from the disclosed date of availability to provide the provider with an oral or written notice of error. The notice must identify: the sender's name and contact information; the recipient's name; the particular transfer; and the reason the sender believes an error occurred. **(§1005.33(b)(1))**

### Provider's Investigation and Remedies

**Investigation timeline:** The provider must investigate promptly and determine whether an error occurred within **90 days** of receiving the notice. **(§1005.33(c)(1))**

**Reporting results:** The provider must report its results to the sender, including notice of any remedies available, within **3 business days** after completing the investigation. **(§1005.33(c)(1))**

**Remedies when error confirmed (§1005.33(c)(2)):**

| Error type | Remedy |
|-----------|--------|
| Incorrect amount, computational error, or amount shortfall (§1005.33(a)(1)(i)–(iii)) | Either: (A) refund to sender the amount appropriate to resolve the error; or (B) make the correct amount available to the recipient — at no additional cost to either party |
| Failure to deliver by disclosed date (§1005.33(a)(1)(iv)) | (A) Same refund or make-available options as above; AND (B) refund all fees and taxes collected on the transfer |
| Incorrect account number provided by sender (§1005.33(a)(1)(iv)(D) exception) | Provider must provide the remedy within 3 business days of the report; may deduct actual fees imposed on the first unsuccessful transfer attempt (but not its own fee) |

All remedies must be carried out within **1 business day** of, or as soon as reasonably practicable after, receiving the sender's instructions about the preferred remedy. **(§1005.33(c)(2))**

**When no error or different error found:** The provider must give the sender a written explanation of its findings and inform the sender of the right to request copies of the documents relied upon. **(§1005.33(d))**

### Incorrect Account Number Exception (§1005.33(h))

A provider is not liable for failure to deliver when the sender provided an incorrect account number or recipient institution identifier, if the provider:

1. Can demonstrate the sender provided an incorrect identifier.
2. Used reasonably available means to verify that the identifier corresponded to the institution name provided.
3. Notified the sender before payment that an incorrect identifier could result in loss of the transfer amount.
4. The incorrect identifier resulted in funds being deposited in an account that is not the designated recipient's account.
5. The provider used reasonable efforts to recover the transfer amount.

### Compliance Program and Recordkeeping (§1005.33(g))

Providers must maintain written policies and procedures to ensure compliance with error resolution requirements, including procedures for retaining:
- Notices of error submitted by senders
- Documentation provided by the sender
- Findings of the provider's error investigation
Record retention requirements under §1005.13 (2 years) apply to all error resolution documentation.

## Cancellation and Refund (§1005.34)

**Mandatory 30-minute cancellation window:** A provider must comply with a sender's request to cancel a remittance transfer if:

1. The request is received by the provider **no later than 30 minutes** after the sender makes payment; and
2. The transferred funds have **not yet been picked up** by the designated recipient and have **not been deposited** into the designated recipient's account. **(§1005.34(a))**

**Refund timeline and content:** The provider must refund, at no additional cost to the sender, the **total amount of funds provided** by the sender — including all fees and taxes — within **3 business days** of receiving the cancellation request. **(§1005.34(b))**

---

## Agent Liability (§1005.35)

A remittance transfer provider is **liable for violations of Subpart B committed by its agents** when the agent acts for the provider. The provider cannot disclaim liability by pointing to the agent's independent actions. **(§1005.35)**

---

## Transfers Scheduled Before the Date of Transfer (§1005.36)

### One-Time Transfers Scheduled 5+ Business Days in Advance

For a one-time transfer scheduled **5 or more business days** before the transfer date, or for the **first** transfer in a series of preauthorised remittance transfers, the provider must:

1. Provide the pre-payment disclosure and receipt (or combined disclosure) in accordance with the timing requirements of §1005.31(e). **(§1005.36(a)(1)(i))**
2. If the disclosures contained permitted estimates: provide an accurate receipt meeting §1005.31(b)(2) requirements within **1 business day** after the actual transfer date. If the transfer comes from the sender's account at the provider, the accurate receipt may be provided on or with the next periodic statement or within **30 days** after the transfer if no periodic statement is issued. **(§1005.36(a)(1)(ii))**

### Subsequent Preauthorised Transfers

For each subsequent preauthorised remittance transfer in a series:

- If information on the most recent receipt is **no longer accurate** (other than temporal disclosures), the provider must send an updated receipt to the sender a **reasonable time before** the next scheduled transfer. **(§1005.36(a)(2)(i))**
- If the disclosures contained no estimates: provide an accurate receipt within **1 business day** after the transfer date (or on next periodic statement / within 30 days). **(§1005.36(a)(2)(ii))**

### Cancellation of Scheduled Transfers (§1005.36(c))

For any remittance transfer scheduled by the sender **at least 3 business days before** the transfer date, a provider must comply with any oral or written cancellation request that:

1. Identifies the sender and the particular transfer to be cancelled.
2. Is received at least **3 business days before** the scheduled date of transfer.

This is broader than the standard 30-minute cancellation window in §1005.34, which applies to immediate transfers.

### Additional Requirements for Subsequent Preauthorised Transfers (§1005.36(d))

For each subsequent transfer in a preauthorised series, the provider must disclose to the sender:

- The **future transfer date** (using the term "Future Transfer Date")
- A **statement of cancellation rights** (as described in §1005.31(b)(2)(iv))
- Provider name, telephone numbers, and website

This disclosure must be received by the sender no more than **12 months** and no less than **5 business days** before the date of the subsequent transfer to which it pertains. **(§1005.36(d)(2)(i))**

## Key Points for Agents

- **Safe harbor is 500 transfers in both years.** The threshold must be met for both the prior calendar year and the current calendar year. A person that exceeded 500 in the prior year is already a "remittance transfer provider" even if they are below 500 in the current year. **(§1005.30(f)(2))**
- **Pre-payment disclosure must show the recipient-currency amount before the sender pays.** The entire disclosure framework — amount, exchange rate, fees, "total to recipient" — must be delivered before payment, not after. This is the critical compliance gate for payment apps and money transfer fintechs. **(§1005.31(b)(1); (e)(1))**
- **Non-covered third-party fees (receiving bank fees) do not have to be exact.** The provider is permanently permitted to estimate receiving-institution fees and must include a prescribed model-language disclaimer. However, covered third-party fees (e.g. correspondent bank fees) must be exact unless a §1005.32 exception applies. **(§§1005.30(h); 1005.32(b)(3))**
- **The 30-minute cancellation right is mandatory and unconditional (subject only to funds not yet received).** A provider cannot contractually waive or shorten it. The only condition on which the right fails is that the designated recipient has already picked up or received the funds. **(§1005.34)**
- **Error investigation window is 90 days; reporting must happen within 3 business days after completion.** These are hard deadlines, not targets. Failure to report results within 3 business days of completing the investigation is itself a Reg E violation. **(§1005.33(c)(1))**
- **Agent violations are the provider's liability.** A fintech operating as an agent of a licensed money transmitter does not shield the provider from liability. The provider's compliance program must cover agent conduct. **(§1005.35)**
- **Preauthorised remittance transfers scheduled 3+ days in advance can be cancelled with 3-day notice.** This is broader than the 30-minute window. Remittance transfer providers must design their systems to accept cancellation requests up to 3 business days before any scheduled transfer date in a preauthorised series. **(§1005.36(c))**
