# BSA — Suspicious Activity Reports for Money Services Businesses

## Overview

§1022.320 establishes the MSB-specific SAR requirements. Every MSB must file a SAR for any suspicious transaction relevant to a possible violation of law or regulation that meets the monetary threshold. MSBs may also voluntarily file SARs for transactions below the threshold or for transactions not otherwise required to be reported but which the MSB believes are relevant to a possible violation of any law or regulation. **(§1022.320(a)(1))**

---

## Filing Threshold — §1022.320(a)(2)

A transaction requires SAR reporting if it is **conducted or attempted, by, at, or through the MSB**, involves or aggregates **at least $2,000 in funds or other assets** (or at least $5,000 for issuers/sellers/redeemers of money orders or traveler's checks that have access to clearance records), and the MSB **knows, suspects, or has reason to suspect** that the transaction:

- **(i)** Involves funds derived from illegal activities or is intended to hide or disguise funds from illegal activities
- **(ii)** Is designed to evade any requirements of the BSA
- **(iii)** Has no business or apparent lawful purpose, and the MSB knows of no reasonable explanation after examining the available facts, including the background and possible purpose of the transaction
- **(iv)** **Involves the use of the MSB to facilitate criminal activity** ← MSB-specific fourth ground absent from the bank SAR provision

**(§1022.320(a)(2))**

### Threshold Comparison Table

| MSB Type | SAR Threshold |
|----------|--------------|
| All MSBs (general) | ≥**$2,000** |
| Issuers, sellers, or redeemers of money orders or traveler's checks with access to clearance records | ≥**$5,000** |
| Banks (§1020.320) | ≥**$5,000** |

---

## Filing Procedures — §1022.320(b)

### What to File

A suspicious transaction is reported by completing a **Suspicious Activity Report (SAR)** and collecting and maintaining supporting documentation. **(§1022.320(b)(1))**

### Where to File

SARs are filed with **FinCEN**, in a central location as indicated in the instructions to the SAR form. **(§1022.320(b)(2))**

### When to File — §1022.320(b)(3)

| Circumstance | Deadline |
|-------------|---------|
| Suspect identified on date of detection | **30 calendar days** from initial detection |
| No suspect identified on date of detection | **60 calendar days** from initial detection to identify a suspect (still file within 60 days even if no suspect is ever identified) |
| Absolute outer limit | **No case shall reporting be delayed more than 60 calendar days** from the date of initial detection |

**Ongoing schemes — immediate telephone notification:** In situations involving violations requiring immediate attention — such as ongoing money laundering schemes — the MSB shall **immediately notify, by telephone, an appropriate law enforcement authority** in addition to filing a timely SAR. **(§1022.320(b)(3))**

**Voluntary terrorist activity reporting:** If an MSB has information that may be relevant to violations involving or relating to terrorist activity, the MSB may call the **FinCEN Hotline at 1-866-556-3974** in addition to filing a required SAR, or to report suspicious activity that does not meet the SAR threshold. **(§1022.320(b)(3))**

---

## Exceptions — §1022.320(c)

An MSB is **not required** to file a SAR for:
- A **robbery or burglary** committed or attempted that is reported to appropriate law enforcement authorities **(§1022.320(c)(1))**
- **Lost, missing, counterfeit, or stolen securities** with respect to which the MSB files a report pursuant to 17 CFR 240.17f-1 **(§1022.320(c)(2))**

---

## Retention of Records — §1022.320(d)

An MSB must retain:
- A **copy of any SAR filed**
- The **original or business record equivalent of any supporting documentation**

Retention period: **5 years from the date of filing the SAR**

Supporting documentation must be:
- Identified and maintained by the MSB as such
- Deemed to have been filed with the SAR
- Made available to FinCEN, any Federal, State, or local law enforcement agency, or any Federal regulatory authority that examines the MSB for BSA compliance, or any State regulatory authority administering a State law requiring BSA compliance — **upon request** **(§1022.320(d))**

---

## Confidentiality — §1022.320(e)

A SAR, and any information that would reveal the existence of a SAR, are confidential. The confidentiality rules for MSB SARs are **structurally identical** to those for bank SARs under §1020.320(e):

- **No MSB, director, officer, employee, or agent** shall disclose a SAR or information revealing its existence **(§1022.320(e)(1))**
- **Subpoena refusal obligation:** Any MSB subpoenaed or otherwise requested to disclose a SAR **shall decline** to produce it, citing §1022.320(e)(1)(i) and 31 USC 5318(g)(2)(A)(i), and must notify FinCEN of the request and response **(§1022.320(e)(1)(i))**
- **Permitted disclosures** (same as banks): to FinCEN/law enforcement/regulators; underlying facts (not the SAR) to another FI for joint SAR preparation; within the corporate organisational structure for BSA purposes **(§1022.320(e)(1)(ii))**
- **Government authority prohibition:** Government authorities may not disclose a SAR in a private legal proceeding (not an "official duty") **(§1022.320(e)(2))**

---

## Safe Harbour — §1022.320(f)

An MSB, and any director, officer, employee, or agent, that makes a voluntary disclosure of any possible violation or makes a disclosure pursuant to §1022.320 is **protected from liability** to any person for any such disclosure or for failure to provide notice of such disclosure — to the **full extent provided by 31 USC 5318(g)(3)**. **(§1022.320(f))**

---

## Examination and Compliance — §1022.320(g)

MSBs shall be examined by FinCEN or its delegatees for compliance with §1022.320. Failure may constitute a violation of the BSA and of Part 1022 of Title 31 CFR. **(§1022.320(g))**

---

## MSB SAR vs Bank SAR

| Feature | MSB SAR (§1022.320) | Bank SAR (§1020.320) |
|---------|---------------------|---------------------|
| General threshold | ≥**$2,000** | ≥**$5,000** |
| Money order/traveler's check issuers | ≥**$5,000** (clearance records) | N/A |
| Filing grounds | **4** (adds "use of MSB to facilitate criminal activity") | 3 |
| Voluntary terrorist reporting | **FinCEN Hotline 1-866-556-3974** | Not separately specified |
| Deadline | 30 days / 60-day absolute limit | Same |
| Ongoing scheme obligation | Immediate telephone notification | Same |
| Exceptions | Robbery/burglary; lost/stolen securities per 17 CFR 240.17f-1 | Same |
| Confidentiality/safe harbour | Identical structure | Same |
| Retention | 5 years from filing | 5 years from filing |

---

## Key Points for Agents

- **The MSB SAR threshold is $2,000 — less than half the bank threshold of $5,000.** Only money order and traveler's check issuers with clearance records use the $5,000 level. Attempted transactions are included in the same way as completed ones. **(§1022.320(a)(2))**
- **MSBs have a fourth SAR filing ground — "involves use of the MSB to facilitate criminal activity."** This ground is unique to MSBs and is absent from the bank SAR provision (§1020.320). It captures scenarios where the MSB itself is the instrument of criminal conduct, not just the transaction. **(§1022.320(a)(2)(iv))**
- **The 30/60-day filing timeline and absolute outer limit are identical to the bank SAR rules.** A 60-day period is only an extension available where no suspect was identified at the date of detection — it is not a general extension. No reporting may be delayed more than 60 calendar days from initial detection under any circumstances. **(§1022.320(b)(3))**
- **Ongoing schemes require immediate telephone notification to law enforcement — this is cumulative with, not an alternative to, filing the SAR.** The MSB must both call law enforcement immediately and file the SAR within the applicable deadline. **(§1022.320(b)(3))**
- **Voluntary terrorist activity reports should go to the FinCEN Hotline (1-866-556-3974), separately from or in addition to required SAR filings.** This hotline is available for reporting below-threshold suspicious activity related to terrorism and for urgent information that cannot wait for the standard SAR process. **(§1022.320(b)(3))**
