# BSA — Information Sharing Under Section 314

## Overview

Section 314 of the USA PATRIOT Act (codified at 31 USC 5318(g)) created two complementary information sharing mechanisms — mandatory (314(a)) and voluntary (314(b)) — to facilitate the exchange of information between government and financial institutions, and between financial institutions themselves, for the purpose of detecting and deterring money laundering and terrorist activity. Both regimes are implemented in 31 CFR Part 1010 Subpart E (§§1010.500–1010.540). **(§1010.500)**

---

## 314(a) — Government-to-Financial Institution Sharing (§1010.520)

### Mechanism

A law enforcement agency investigating terrorist activity or money laundering may request that FinCEN **compel financial institutions to search their records** for accounts or transactions involving named individuals, entities, or organisations. **(§1010.520(b)(1))**

### Who Can Initiate

A **law enforcement agency** means a federal, state, local, or foreign law enforcement agency with criminal investigative authority. Foreign law enforcement agencies qualify only if they are from a jurisdiction that:
- Is a party to a treaty providing reciprocal access; or
- Is a jurisdiction that otherwise allows US law enforcement agencies reciprocal access to comparable information **(§1010.520(a)(2))**

FinCEN may also initiate requests on its own behalf and on behalf of appropriate Treasury components. **(§1010.520(b)(2))**

### The Request Process

1. The law enforcement agency submits a **written certification** to FinCEN that:
   - Each named individual, entity, or organisation is engaged in, or is reasonably suspected based on credible evidence of engaging in, terrorist activity or money laundering
   - Includes enough specific identifiers (date of birth, address, SSN) to allow differentiation between common or similar names
   - Identifies one person at the agency who can be contacted about the request **(§1010.520(b)(1))**
2. FinCEN transmits the request to covered financial institutions

### Financial Institution Obligations Upon Receiving a 314(a) Request

**Record search (§1010.520(b)(3)(i)):**
The FI must **expeditiously search its records** to determine whether it maintains or has maintained accounts for, or has engaged in transactions with, each named individual, entity, or organisation. Specifically:
- **Current accounts** maintained for a named suspect
- **Accounts** maintained for a named suspect during the **preceding 12 months**
- **Transactions** conducted by or on behalf of a named suspect, or transmittals where the named suspect was transmitter or recipient, during the **preceding 6 months** that are required by law or regulation to be recorded or that are electronically maintained

**Report to FinCEN (§1010.520(b)(3)(ii)):**
If the FI identifies an account or transaction matching a named individual, entity, or organisation, it must report to FinCEN:
- The name of such individual, entity, or organisation
- The number of each such account (or, for transactions, the date and type of each such transaction)
- Any Social Security number, taxpayer identification number, passport number, date of birth, address, or other similar identifying information

**Designate a contact person (§1010.520(b)(3)(iii)):**
Upon receiving a 314(a) request, the FI must designate one person to be the **point of contact** for the institution regarding the request and to receive similar future requests. The FI must provide FinCEN with the name, title, mailing address, email address, telephone number, and fax number of such person. The FI must promptly notify FinCEN of any changes to this contact information.

### Use and Security of 314(a) Information (§1010.520(b)(3)(iv))

A financial institution receiving a 314(a) request **must not use the information for any purpose other than:**
1. Reporting to FinCEN as required
2. Determining whether to establish or maintain an account, or to engage in a transaction
3. Assisting the financial institution in complying with any BSA requirement

The FI **must not disclose** the fact that FinCEN has requested or obtained information to any person, except:
- To FinCEN, the requesting Treasury component, or the law enforcement agency on whose behalf FinCEN is requesting information
- To another FI that is authorised to share information under §1010.540 (314(b)), where the disclosure concerns an individual/entity/organisation named in the request

**Confidentiality of request:** A financial institution must not disclose to the subject of a 314(a) request the existence or terms of the request. **(§1010.520(b)(3)(iv)(B)(1))**

### No Mandatory Action Required (§1010.520(b)(5))

Nothing in §1010.520 requires a FI to:
- Take any action (or to decline to take any action) with respect to an account or transaction involving a named suspect
- Treat a 314(a) suspect list as a government list for purposes of USA PATRIOT Act §326 (CIP rules)

A 314(a) request does not require future account opening reports or monitoring. **(§1010.520(b)(5))**

### Legal Framework

Information that a FI is required to report under §1010.520(b)(3)(ii) is required to be reported in accordance with a Federal statute, for purposes of:
- 12 USC 3413(d) (Right to Financial Privacy Act exception)
- 15 USC 6802(e)(8) (Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act exception)
**(§1010.520(b)(4))**

## 314(b) — Voluntary Financial Institution-to-Financial Institution Sharing (§1010.540)

### Purpose and Safe Harbour

Under 314(b), a financial institution may voluntarily **share information with other financial institutions** regarding individuals, entities, organisations, and countries suspected of possible involvement in terrorist activity or money laundering. The financial institution that shares the information is **protected from liability** for such sharing, provided it complies with the procedural requirements. **(§1010.540(b)(1), (5))**

### Who May Participate

A financial institution may participate in 314(b) sharing if it:
1. Is required under Part 1010 to establish and maintain an AML programme, or is treated as having satisfied the AML programme requirement under 31 USC 5318(h)(1); and
2. Has not been designated by FinCEN as ineligible to share information under §1010.540 **(§1010.540(a)(1))**

Associations of financial institutions (groups or organisations whose membership is comprised entirely of eligible financial institutions) may also participate. **(§1010.540(a)(2))**

### Notice Requirement (§1010.540(b)(2))

A FI that intends to share information under 314(b) must submit a **notice to FinCEN** via FinCEN's Internet Web site. Each notice is **effective for one year** from the date FinCEN receives it. FIs must submit a new notice to continue sharing after the one-year period.

### Verification Requirement (§1010.540(b)(3))

Prior to sharing information, a FI must take **reasonable steps to verify** that the other FI (or association) with which it intends to share:
- Has submitted a notice to FinCEN under §1010.540(b)(2)

A FI may satisfy this requirement by:
- Confirming that the other institution appears on a list that FinCEN makes periodically available to filers; or
- Confirming directly with the other institution that the requisite notice has been filed

### Permitted Uses of 314(b) Information (§1010.540(b)(4))

Information received through 314(b) sharing may only be used for:
1. **Identifying and, where appropriate, reporting** ML or terrorist activities
2. **Determining whether to establish or maintain** an account or to engage in a transaction
3. **Assisting the FI in complying** with any BSA requirement

### Confidentiality of 314(b) Information (§1010.540(b)(4)(ii))

Each FI participating in 314(b) must maintain **adequate procedures to protect the security and confidentiality** of shared information. A FI satisfies this requirement to the extent it applies the same information procedures it has established to satisfy Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act §501 requirements for protecting customers' nonpublic personal information (15 USC 6801). **(§1010.540(b)(4)(ii))**

### SAR Filing Obligation (§1010.540(c))

If, as a result of information shared under 314(b), a FI knows, suspects, or has reason to suspect that an individual, entity, or organisation is involved in, or may be involved in, terrorist activity or money laundering, and the FI is subject to SAR filing requirements, **it must file a SAR** in accordance with those regulations.

In situations involving violations requiring immediate attention (e.g., ongoing terrorist activity), the FI must also **immediately notify, by telephone**, an appropriate law enforcement authority and its financial institution supervisory authorities, in addition to filing a SAR. **(§1010.540(c))**

**Note:** A FI not subject to SAR filing requirements is not required to notify law enforcement, but is **encouraged to voluntarily report** suspicious activity detected through 314(b) sharing. **(§1010.540(c))**

### Safe Harbour Limitations (§1010.540(b)(5)(ii))

The safe harbour does not apply if the FI fails to comply with the notice requirement (§1010.540(b)(2)), the verification requirement (§1010.540(b)(3)), or the permitted use/confidentiality requirements (§1010.540(b)(4)).

## Comparison: 314(a) vs 314(b)

| Feature | 314(a) — §1010.520 | 314(b) — §1010.540 |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Initiator | FinCEN on behalf of law enforcement | Financial institutions themselves |
| Direction | Government → FI | FI → FI |
| Voluntary? | No — mandatory compliance | Yes — voluntary |
| Who receives? | FinCEN (and law enforcement) | Other financial institutions |
| Subject matter | Named specific suspects | Suspected ML/terrorist activity generally |
| Safe harbour from liability? | N/A (required disclosure) | Yes — if procedural requirements met |
| Annual notice to FinCEN required? | No | Yes — notice required; effective one year |
| Use restrictions? | Yes — strictly limited to reporting, account/transaction decisions, BSA compliance | Yes — restricted to ML/terrorism identification, account/transaction decisions, BSA compliance |
| Confidentiality? | Yes — cannot disclose request to subject | Yes — must maintain adequate security procedures |
| SAR obligation triggered? | Separate determination required | Yes — if known/suspected ML or terrorist activity and FI has SAR obligation |

## Key Points for Agents

- **314(a) is mandatory; failure to search and respond is a BSA violation.** When FinCEN transmits a 314(a) request, the FI must expeditiously search records covering current accounts, accounts from the preceding 12 months, and transactions/transmittals from the preceding 6 months. The FI designates a contact person and reports any match to FinCEN. **(§1010.520(b)(1), (3))**
- **314(b) sharing requires an annual FinCEN notice — without it, the safe harbour does not apply.** The notice is filed via FinCEN's website, is effective for one year, and must be renewed. FIs must also verify that counterpart FIs have filed before sharing. **(§1010.540(b)(2)–(3))**
- **314(a) information cannot be used as a blanket watchlist.** A 314(a) suspect list is not a government list for CIP purposes under USA PATRIOT Act §326. The FI cannot use 314(a) information to indefinitely monitor accounts or report on future transactions. **(§1010.520(b)(5))**
- **314(b) sharing that reveals suspected ML or terrorism creates a SAR filing obligation.** The voluntary sharing does not eliminate the FI's existing SAR obligations — if sharing produces reasonable grounds to suspect ML or terrorist activity, the FI must file a SAR and (for urgent situations) immediately notify law enforcement by telephone. **(§1010.540(c))**
- **Both regimes have strict confidentiality requirements.** 314(a): FI cannot disclose the request or its existence to the named suspects; disclosure is only permitted to FinCEN, the requesting law enforcement agency, or 314(b) sharing partners. 314(b): FI must apply GLB-equivalent security procedures to all received information. **(§§1010.520(b)(3)(iv), 1010.540(b)(4)(ii))**
