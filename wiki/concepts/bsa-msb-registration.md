# BSA — MSB Registration with FinCEN

## Overview

§1022.380 requires every MSB (with limited exceptions) to register with FinCEN. Registration is a distinct obligation from the AML programme, SAR filing, and recordkeeping requirements of Part 1022 — it is the threshold obligation that puts the MSB in the FinCEN registration system. Failure to register is itself a criminal offence under 18 USC 1960 (operating an unregistered money transmitting business). **(§1022.380)**

---

## Who Must Register — §1022.380(a)

Every **money services business** as defined in §1010.100(ff) must register with FinCEN, **except:**

1. **Agents acting solely as an agent for another MSB** — a person who acts as an MSB solely as an agent for one or more other MSBs (the principal(s)) is exempt from registration, provided the principal MSB(s) register and maintain the agent list required by §1022.380(d)
2. **The United States Postal Service (USPS)**

All other MSBs — including currency dealers/exchangers, check cashers, issuers/sellers/redeemers of traveler's checks and money orders, providers/sellers of prepaid access, and money transmitters — must register. **(§1022.380(a))**

---

## When to Register — §1022.380(b)

### New MSBs

An MSB must register with FinCEN within **180 days** of the date the business is established. **(§1022.380(b)(1))**

### Re-registration

An MSB that has registered must re-register **every 2 years**. The 2-year period is calculated from the date of initial registration or the most recent re-registration. **(§1022.380(b)(2))**

### Mandatory Re-registration Events — §1022.380(b)(3)

In addition to the biennial re-registration cycle, an MSB must re-register upon any of the following events:

| Triggering Event | Re-registration Timing |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| Change in ownership that results in a new controlling person | Within **180 days** of the change |
| Transfer of >**10%** of equity interest in the business | Within **180 days** of the transfer |
| Increase in the number of agents by more than **50%** since the most recent registration | Within **180 days** of reaching the 50%+ threshold |

**(§1022.380(b)(3))**

---

## How to Register — §1022.380(c)

Registration is filed with FinCEN on the form prescribed by FinCEN. The registration form must include:

- The name of the business and any trade name
- The address of the business
- The name and address of any owner or controlling person
- The type(s) of money services business
- A list of agents (see §1022.380(d) for agent list requirements)
- Any other information FinCEN prescribes by form or regulation

**(§1022.380(c))**

---

## Agent List — §1022.380(d)

Each registered MSB that uses agents (persons acting as its agent in the conduct of MSB activities) must:

1. **Maintain a list of its agents** — including: the name and address of each agent; the type of MSB activity conducted; whether the agent is incorporated/organised under US law or state law; and the agent's TIN
2. **Update the list annually** — the list must be prepared and maintained as of January 1 each year and be available to FinCEN upon request within 10 business days

**(§1022.380(d))**

---

## Penalties for Non-Registration — §1022.380(e)

| Type | Penalty |
|------|---------|
| Civil penalty | Up to **$5,000 per day** for each day of non-registration |
| Criminal penalty | Under **18 USC 1960** — operating an unlicensed/unregistered money transmitting business; up to 5 years imprisonment |

**(§1022.380(e))**

**Note:** Criminal liability under 18 USC 1960 does not require intent to violate BSA registration requirements — the failure to register is itself the criminal act.

---

## Relationship to BOI Reporting

MSB registration with FinCEN under §1022.380 is **distinct from** and does **not satisfy** the Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) reporting requirement under §1010.380 (Corporate Transparency Act). MSBs that are not otherwise exempt from BOI reporting must separately comply with §1010.380. The two registration systems serve different purposes: §1022.380 is an MSB regulatory licence; §1010.380 creates a database of beneficial ownership for law enforcement and intelligence. **(§§1022.380, 1010.380)**

---

## Key Points for Agents

- **Every MSB must register within 180 days of establishment — except pure agents (acting solely for one principal MSB) and USPS.** A person operating as an MSB without registration is committing a criminal offence under 18 USC 1960, regardless of whether any money laundering has occurred. **(§1022.380(a)–(b))**
- **Re-registration is required every 2 years and upon three specific triggering events:** (1) ownership change creating a new controlling person; (2) >10% equity transfer; (3) >50% increase in agents. All three trigger re-registration within 180 days. **(§1022.380(b))**
- **The civil penalty for non-registration is $5,000 per day — and this is a continuing violation.** An MSB that fails to register for a year faces potential penalties exceeding $1.8M before any other BSA violations are considered. **(§1022.380(e))**
- **The annual agent list is a separate ongoing obligation, not just a registration one-time requirement.** The list must be updated as of January 1 each year and must be available to FinCEN within 10 business days of request. **(§1022.380(d))**
- **MSB registration does not substitute for BOI reporting under §1010.380.** MSBs that are registering companies under the Corporate Transparency Act must comply with both regimes separately. **(§§1022.380, 1010.380)**
