# Reg Z — Closed-End Credit Disclosures (Subpart C)

## Overview

Subpart C of Regulation Z (12 CFR §§1026.17–1026.24) governs disclosure obligations for **closed-end consumer credit** — any consumer credit that is not open-end. Closed-end credit is repaid on a fixed schedule with a defined total obligation. **(§1026.2(a)(10); by exclusion from §1026.2(a)(20))**

**Products covered:** Instalment loans, auto loans, personal loans, buy-now-pay-later (BNPL) products (where structured as instalment plans), student loans (other than those with specific private education loan rules under Subpart F), and most mortgage transactions.

**Mortgage-specific rules:** Residential mortgage transactions have additional overlay requirements under §1026.19 (Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure timing; fee restrictions; good faith determination), §1026.20 (post-consummation ARM adjustment notices), §1026.23 (right of rescission for non-purchase transactions), and Subpart E (high-cost and higher-priced mortgage loan requirements).

## General Disclosure Requirements — §1026.17

### Form — §1026.17(a)

Closed-end disclosures must be:
- **Clear and conspicuous** in all respects.
- **In writing** and in a form the consumer can keep.
- Electronic form is permitted subject to compliance with the E-Sign Act (15 USC 7001 et seq.).
- The **finance charge** and **annual percentage rate** must be disclosed **more conspicuously** than any other required disclosure — typically by bolder or larger typeface. **(§1026.17(a)(2))**
- Disclosures must be **grouped together**, segregated from everything else, and must not contain any information not directly related to the required disclosures (with specified exceptions such as the creditor's identity, variable-rate examples, and insurance disclosures).

### Timing — §1026.17(b)

The creditor must make disclosures **before consummation** of the transaction — i.e., before the consumer becomes contractually obligated. Consummation is when the consumer signs the credit agreement. **(§1026.2(a)(13))**

Exceptions:
- Mail/phone orders: the creditor may delay disclosures until the due date of the first payment if specific summary information (loan amount, finance charge, APR, terms of repayment) was made available in writing before the order was placed. **(§1026.17(g))**
- Mortgage early disclosures under §1026.19 impose earlier timing for certain residential mortgage transactions.

### Basis of Disclosures — §1026.17(c)

- Disclosures must reflect the terms of the **legal obligation** between the parties.
- If a term is unknown to the creditor at the time of disclosure, the creditor shall disclose based on the **best information reasonably available** and must clearly state that the disclosure is an estimate. **(§1026.17(c)(2)(i))**
- For per-diem interest, any disclosure affected by per-diem interest is considered accurate if based on the information known to the creditor when the disclosure documents are prepared.

### Subsequent Events — §1026.17(e)

If a disclosure becomes inaccurate after delivery because of a subsequent event, the inaccuracy is not a violation — but new disclosures may be required under §1026.19 or §1026.48(c)(4).

### Early Disclosures — §1026.17(f)

For non-mortgage closed-end transactions, if disclosures are given before consummation and a subsequent event makes them inaccurate:
- If the APR at consummation varies by more than 1/8 of 1 percentage point from the disclosed APR (or 1/4 of 1 percentage point for irregular transactions), new disclosures must be provided.
- Any changed term (other than the APR within the above tolerance) that was based on an estimate and labelled as such does not require redisclosure.

## Content of Closed-End Disclosures — §1026.18 (The TILA Box)

For each closed-end transaction (other than a RESPA-covered mortgage subject to §1026.19(e) and (f)), the creditor must disclose the following items, to the extent applicable:

| Item | Statutory label | What must be disclosed |
|------|----------------|----------------------|
| **(a) Creditor** | — | The identity of the creditor making the disclosures. |
| **(b) Amount financed** | "Amount Financed" with brief description ("the amount of credit provided to you or on your behalf") | Principal loan amount minus any prepaid finance charges, plus any amounts financed by the creditor that are not part of the finance charge. **(§1026.18(b))** |
| **(c) Itemisation of amount financed** | — | A separate written itemisation showing: proceeds to the consumer; amount credited to consumer's account; amounts paid to third parties on the consumer's behalf; the prepaid finance charge. The creditor may alternatively state the consumer's right to receive the itemisation and omit it unless the consumer requests it. **(§1026.18(c))** |
| **(d) Finance charge** | "Finance Charge" with description ("the dollar amount the credit will cost you") | The finance charge as a dollar amount. For mortgage loans: accurate if understated by no more than $100; for other credit: accurate if understated/overstated by no more than $5 (for amounts financed ≤$1,000) or $10 (for amounts financed >$1,000). **(§1026.18(d))** |
| **(e) Annual percentage rate** | "Annual Percentage Rate" with description ("the cost of your credit as a yearly rate") | The APR calculated per §1026.22 and appendix J. Not required if finance charge is $5 or less on an amount financed ≤$75, or $7.50 or less on an amount financed >$75. **(§1026.18(e))** |
| **(f) Variable rate** | — | If the APR may increase after consummation: the circumstances under which it may increase; any limitations on the increase; the effect of an increase; and an example of the payment terms that would result from an increase. For variable-rate dwelling-secured transactions with term >1 year, reference to earlier variable-rate disclosures is permitted in lieu of full disclosure. **(§1026.18(f))** |
| **(g) Payment schedule** | — | The number, amounts, and timing of payments scheduled to repay the obligation. If payments vary because the finance charge is applied to the unpaid principal balance, the creditor may instead disclose the dollar amounts of the largest and smallest payments and a reference to the variations. **(§1026.18(g))** |
| **(h) Total of payments** | "Total of Payments" with description ("the amount you will have paid when you have made all scheduled payments") | Sum of all scheduled payments. Not required for single-payment transactions. **(§1026.18(h))** |
| **(i) Demand feature** | — | If the obligation has a demand feature (repayable on demand), that fact. **(§1026.18(i))** |
| **(j) Total sale price** | "Total Sale Price" with descriptive explanation | For credit sales: the total of the downpayment, other payments, and the finance charge. **(§1026.18(j))** |
| **(k) Prepayment** | — | (1) Whether a prepayment penalty may be charged if the loan's principal is paid before due date. (2) Whether the consumer is entitled to a rebate of the finance charge if prepaid in full or in part. **(§1026.18(k))** |
| **(l) Late payment** | — | Any dollar or percentage charge that may be imposed for a late payment (other than a deferral or extension charge). **(§1026.18(l))** |
| **(m) Security interest** | — | The fact that the creditor has or will acquire a security interest in the property purchased or in other property identified by type. **(§1026.18(m))** |
| **(n) Insurance and debt cancellation** | — | Items required by §1026.4(d) to exclude certain insurance premiums and debt cancellation fees from the finance charge. **(§1026.18(n))** |
| **(o) Certain security interest charges** | — | Items required by §1026.4(e) to exclude certain fees from the finance charge. **(§1026.18(o))** |
| **(p) Contract reference** | — | Statement that the consumer should refer to the contract for information about nonpayment, default, the right to accelerate maturity, and prepayment rebates and penalties. **(§1026.18(p))** |
| **(q) Assumption policy** | — | For residential mortgage transactions: whether a subsequent purchaser may assume the remaining obligation on its original terms. **(§1026.18(q))** |
| **(r) Required deposit** | — | If the creditor requires the consumer to maintain a deposit as a condition of the specific transaction: a statement that the APR does not reflect the effect of the required deposit. **(§1026.18(r))** |
| **(s) Interest rate and payment summary** | Tabular format | For closed-end mortgage transactions (other than RESPA-covered mortgages under §1026.19(e)/(f)): a table disclosing (i) the interest rate at consummation; (ii) for adjustable/step-rate mortgages, the maximum interest rate in the first 5 years and the earliest date it may apply; (iii) the maximum interest rate over the life of the loan and the earliest date; and corresponding payment amounts. Must be in at least 10-point font in a table substantially similar to Model Clause H-4 formats. **(§1026.18(s))** |
| **(t) No-guarantee-to-refinance statement** | — | For closed-end mortgages secured by real property or a dwelling (other than RESPA-covered mortgages): a statement that there is no guarantee the consumer can refinance the transaction to lower the interest rate or periodic payments. Format substantially similar to Model Clause H-4(K). **(§1026.18(t))** |

## Mortgage-Specific Timing and Process — §1026.19

### RESPA-Covered Mortgage Transactions — §1026.19(e)

For closed-end consumer credit transactions secured by **real property** (other than reverse mortgages), Subpart C imposes a distinct two-stage disclosure process aligned with RESPA:

#### Stage 1 — Loan Estimate (§1026.37)

**Who provides it:** The creditor (or the mortgage broker, with the creditor responsible for compliance).

**When:** Within **3 business days** after the creditor receives the consumer's application. **(§1026.19(e)(1)(iii)(A))**

**Earliest consummation:** The Loan Estimate must be received by the consumer no later than **7 business days before consummation**. **(§1026.19(e)(1)(iii)(B))**

**Fee restriction:** From the time of application until the consumer receives the Loan Estimate and communicates intent to proceed, **no fee may be imposed** on the consumer for any purpose other than a bona fide and reasonable credit report fee. This applies to application fees, rate lock fees, and any other charges. **(§1026.19(e)(2)(i))**

**Good faith determination:** A charge disclosed on the Loan Estimate is in good faith if the actual amount charged does not exceed the estimated amount. Three tolerance tiers apply **(§1026.19(e)(3))**:
- **Zero tolerance**: Creditor charges, transfer taxes, own-affiliate fees. The actual amount charged cannot exceed the estimated amount.
- **10% tolerance**: Third-party service providers on the creditor's list; recording fees. The aggregate actual charges may exceed the aggregate estimated charges by no more than 10%.
- **No tolerance**: Third-party services for which the consumer is permitted to shop and selects a provider not on the creditor's list; prepaid interest; property insurance premiums.

**Revised Loan Estimates** may be issued when a valid changed circumstance occurs, including **(§1026.19(e)(3)(iv))**:
- Changed circumstances affecting settlement charges (extraordinary events outside the control of any party; new information about the consumer or transaction)
- Changed circumstances affecting eligibility (changed creditworthiness or value of the security)
- Consumer-requested revisions to credit terms
- Interest rate not locked at time of initial Loan Estimate — revised estimate may be issued within 3 business days of rate lock
- Consumer indicating intent to proceed more than 10 business days after delivery of the Loan Estimate

A revised Loan Estimate cannot be delivered on or after the date the Closing Disclosure is delivered. **(§1026.19(e)(4)(ii))**

#### Stage 2 — Closing Disclosure (§1026.38)

**When:** The consumer must receive the Closing Disclosure **no later than 3 business days before consummation**. **(§1026.19(f)(1)(ii)(A))**

**Content:** The Closing Disclosure reflects the actual terms of the transaction and uses the same format and items as the Loan Estimate, updated to reflect final amounts.

**Timing coordination:** The creditor cannot provide a revised Loan Estimate on or after the date the Closing Disclosure is delivered.

### Early Disclosures — Written Estimate Warning — §1026.19(e)(2)(ii)

If any person provides the consumer with a written estimate of terms or costs before the consumer receives the Loan Estimate, the estimate must display the following statement on the **top of the front of the first page** in at least 12-point type:

> "Your actual rate, payment, and costs could be higher. Get an official Loan Estimate before choosing a loan."

The written estimate may not be made with headings or format substantially similar to the Loan Estimate form.

## Right of Rescission — §1026.23

### Scope — §1026.23(a)

The right of rescission applies to any **closed-end consumer credit transaction** in which a **security interest is or will be retained or acquired in the consumer's principal dwelling**, **except**:
- A residential mortgage transaction (purchase money mortgage used to acquire the dwelling)
- A refinancing or consolidation by the same creditor of an extension of credit already secured by the consumer's principal dwelling, to the extent no new money is advanced and the security is not increased
- A transaction in which a state agency is the creditor
- Advances in a series covered by an existing open-end plan that has already been subject to the right of rescission

The right of rescission is primarily relevant to **home equity loans, non-purchase home improvement loans, and refinancings by a new creditor** secured by the consumer's primary residence.

### Exercise Period — §1026.23(a)(3)

The consumer may rescind until **midnight of the third business day** after the **latest** of:
1. Consummation of the transaction
2. Delivery of the rescission notice required by §1026.23(b)
3. Delivery of all material disclosures

**"Material disclosures"** for this purpose means: APR, finance charge, amount financed, total of payments, payment schedule, and the disclosures and limitations required by §§1026.32(c) and (d) and 1026.43(g). **(§1026.23(a)(3)(ii))**

**Extended right — 3 years:** If the required rescission notice or any of the material disclosures **are not delivered**, the consumer's right to rescind does not expire at 3 days — it extends until **3 years after consummation**, or upon the transfer of all of the consumer's interest in the property, or upon sale of the property, whichever occurs first. **(§1026.23(a)(3)(i))**

This is one of the most significant long-tail liabilities in consumer credit law. A creditor that fails to deliver a proper rescission notice on a home equity loan faces a rescission exposure that does not expire for 3 years.

### Notice Requirements — §1026.23(b)

The creditor must deliver **two copies** of the rescission notice to each consumer entitled to rescind (or one copy if delivered electronically with proper E-Sign consent). The notice must be on a separate document that identifies the transaction and clearly discloses:
- The retention or acquisition of a security interest in the consumer's principal dwelling
- The consumer's right to rescind the transaction
- How to exercise the right, with a form and the address of the creditor's place of business
- The effects of rescission
- The date on which the rescission period expires

Model forms in appendix H to Part 1026 (Model Form H-8 and H-9) provide compliant notice forms. **(§1026.23(b)(2))**

### Delay of Creditor's Performance — §1026.23(c)

Until the rescission period has expired and the creditor is reasonably satisfied that the consumer has not rescinded, **no money may be disbursed** (other than into escrow), **no services may be performed**, and **no materials may be delivered** in connection with the transaction. Disbursing loan proceeds before the 3-day rescission period expires violates §1026.23(c).

### Effects of Rescission — §1026.23(d)

When a consumer rescinds:
1. The **security interest becomes void** — the creditor has no lien on the property.
2. The consumer is **not liable for any amount**, including any finance charge.
3. Within **20 calendar days** of receiving a rescission notice, the creditor must return any money or property given in connection with the transaction and take all action necessary to reflect the termination of the security interest.
4. Once the creditor has complied with (3), the consumer must tender any money or property received from the creditor. If tender in money would be impracticable, the consumer may tender the reasonable value of the property or make tender at the location of the property.

### Waiver of Rescission Right — §1026.23(e)

The consumer may waive the right to rescind only if the extension of credit is needed to meet a **bona fide personal financial emergency**. The waiver requires a dated written statement signed by all consumers entitled to rescind, specifically describing the emergency and modifying or waiving the rescission period. Pre-printed waiver forms are prohibited.

### APR Accuracy for Rescission Purposes — §1026.23(g)

For rescission purposes (determining whether material disclosures were delivered), the disclosed finance charge and APR are considered accurate if the disclosed finance charge is:
- Understated by no more than **1/2 of 1 percent** of the face amount of the note or $100 (whichever is greater); or
- Greater than the amount required to be disclosed.

For refinancings with a new creditor where there is no new advance: the tolerance is **1 percent** of the face amount or $100. **(§1026.23(g)(2))**

## Post-Consummation Disclosures — §1026.20

### Refinancings — §1026.20(a)

A refinancing occurs when an existing closed-end obligation is satisfied and replaced by a new obligation by the same consumer. A refinancing is treated as a new transaction requiring new §1026.18 disclosures. The following do not constitute a refinancing requiring new disclosures:
- A renewal of a single payment obligation with no change in original terms
- A reduction in the APR with a corresponding change in the payment schedule
- An agreement involving a court proceeding
- A change in the payment schedule or collateral requirements resulting from the consumer's default (unless the rate is increased or new money is advanced)
- Renewal of optional insurance that was part of the original transaction

### ARM Rate Adjustment Notices — §1026.20(c) and (d)

**Ongoing adjustment notice (§1026.20(c)):** For adjustable-rate mortgages with a term greater than one year, the creditor, assignee, or servicer must provide the consumer with a disclosure at least **60 but no more than 120 days** before the first payment at the new level is due whenever an interest rate adjustment results in a corresponding payment change. The disclosure must include:
- A statement that the interest rate period is ending and the rate and payment will change
- The current and new interest rates
- The current and new payments
- How the new interest rate is determined (index, margin, caps)
- Any limits on the rate or payment increase over the life of the loan
- How the new payment is determined (including loan balance, remaining term)
- A statement whether the new payment will not pay down principal (for interest-only or negatively-amortising scenarios)

**Initial adjustment notice (§1026.20(d)):** For the first ARM interest rate adjustment, the notice must be provided **at least 210 but no more than 240 days** before the first payment at the adjusted level is due. If the first payment at the adjusted level is due within the first 210 days after consummation, the disclosures are provided at consummation.

## Closed-End Advertising — §1026.24

Any advertisement for closed-end credit must comply with the following:

**Only available terms (§1026.24(a)):** An advertisement may not state credit terms that are not actually available from or arranged by the creditor.

**Triggering terms (§1026.24(d)):** If an advertisement states any of the following "trigger terms," it must also disclose the APR (and whether the rate may increase), the amount of the downpayment, the terms of repayment, and the total of payments:
- The amount of any payment
- The number of payments
- The period of repayment
- The amount of any finance charge

**"Fixed" rate prohibition (§1026.24(f)(2)):** An advertisement may not describe a rate as "fixed" unless it also specifies the period for which the rate will be fixed.

**APR required with trigger terms:** Any advertised rate must be stated as an APR using that term. A periodic rate alone may not be advertised without the corresponding APR.

## Key Points for Agents

- **Pre-consummation timing is non-negotiable.** Closed-end disclosures must be in the consumer's hands before consummation. For RESPA mortgages, the Loan Estimate must be received at least 7 business days before consummation and the Closing Disclosure at least 3 business days before. Consummating the transaction before these waiting periods expire is a violation. **(§§1026.17(b); 1026.19(e)(1)(iii)(B); 1026.19(f)(1)(ii))**
- **Finance charge and APR must be the most conspicuous disclosures.** These two items must stand out from all other required disclosures by bolder or larger typeface. A TILA box that does not make these items visually prominent is non-compliant regardless of whether the amounts are accurate. **(§1026.17(a)(2))**
- **Failure to deliver the rescission notice turns a 3-day window into a 3-year window.** For any non-purchase-money transaction secured by a consumer's principal dwelling, failing to deliver the right of rescission notice or any material disclosure extends the rescission right to 3 years from consummation. The two-copy requirement applies — one copy is insufficient. **(§1026.23(a)(3))**
- **No disbursement before the rescission period expires.** For transactions subject to the right of rescission, money cannot be disbursed until the 3-day period has passed and the consumer has not rescinded. Disbursing on the same day as consummation violates §1026.23(c).
- **BNPL instalment products need closed-end disclosures.** A 0% BNPL product payable in 4+ instalments is a closed-end transaction. It requires a TILA box with amount financed, finance charge (even if $0), APR (if applicable), payment schedule, and total of payments before the consumer becomes contractually obligated. **(§§1026.1(c)(1)(iii); 1026.17; 1026.18)**
- **Good faith on the Loan Estimate is measured against the actual charge at consummation.** For zero-tolerance items (creditor charges, transfer taxes), the actual charge cannot exceed the estimated charge by any amount. For 10%-tolerance items (third-party services on the creditor's list), the aggregate of actuals may exceed the aggregate of estimates by up to 10%. Exceeding these tolerances requires the creditor to cure the excess at or before consummation. **(§1026.19(e)(3))**
- **ARM adjustment notices have specific lead times.** Ongoing ARM rate adjustment notices must be delivered 60–120 days before the first payment at the new rate. The initial rate adjustment notice must be delivered 210–240 days before the first adjusted payment. Failing to provide these notices — or providing them outside the required window — is a §1026.20 violation.
- **A refinancing by the same creditor does not automatically require new rescission rights.** If the same creditor refinances an existing obligation secured by the consumer's principal dwelling with no new money advanced and no increase in the security interest, the right of rescission does not apply to the refinancing. A different creditor replacing the existing creditor does not get this exclusion. **(§1026.23(f)(2))**
