# Reg Z — Open-End Credit Disclosures (Subpart B)

## Overview

Subpart B of Regulation Z (12 CFR §§1026.5–1026.16) governs disclosure obligations for open-end consumer credit — credit plans that contemplate repeated transactions, where a finance charge may be imposed on the outstanding balance from time to time, and where the balance may be repaid and re-borrowed up to a pre-set limit. **(§1026.2(a)(20))**

**Products covered:** Credit cards, charge cards, home-equity lines of credit (HELOCs), and any other revolving consumer credit facility.

**Products not covered:** Closed-end instalments (personal loans, auto loans, BNPL) — those fall under Subpart C, regardless of how they are marketed.

Subpart B has three disclosure trigger points: (1) account opening, (2) each billing cycle, and (3) material changes to account terms. A separate advertising regime (§1026.16) governs what may be communicated before the consumer opens an account.

---

## Account-Opening Disclosures — §1026.6

Before a consumer becomes obligated on an open-end credit plan, the creditor must provide the account-opening disclosures. The content and format requirements differ between home-equity plans (§1026.6(a)) and all other open-end plans (§1026.6(b)).

### Home-Equity Plans — §1026.6(a)

For HELOCs subject to §1026.40, the account-opening disclosures must include:

- The circumstances under which a finance charge will be imposed and how it is determined, including when finance charges begin to accrue and whether a grace period exists
- Each periodic rate used to compute the finance charge, the range of balances to which each applies, and the corresponding APR
- How the balance on which the finance charge is computed is determined
- Any other charges (non-finance charge fees) that may be imposed
- The conditions under which the creditor may take certain actions (reduce the credit line, suspend advances, require repayment)
- Payment information for both the draw period and any repayment period
- A statement that negative amortisation may occur (where applicable)
- Variable-rate disclosures as required by §1026.40(d)
- A billing rights statement substantially similar to Model Form G-3

### Non-Home-Secured Open-End Plans (Including Credit Cards) — §1026.6(b)

For credit cards and other non-home-secured revolving plans, the account-opening disclosures must be provided in a **tabular format** (the Schumer Box) with the headings, content, and format substantially similar to the applicable tables in appendix G-17. **(§1026.6(b)(1))**

**Format requirements:**
- **APRs must be disclosed in at least 16-point type** in the table, with one exception: a penalty rate that may apply upon specific events may be in smaller type. **(§1026.6(b)(1)(i))**
- Fees must be disclosed in bold text.
- Only information required or permitted by §1026.6(b)(1) through (v) shall appear in the table itself; additional disclosures go directly below the table or in the account agreement.

**Required content of the account-opening table (§1026.6(b)(2)):**

| Item | What must be disclosed |
|------|----------------------|
| **Annual percentage rate** | Each periodic rate that may compute the finance charge expressed as an APR. Where more than one rate applies, the range of balances to which each applies. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(i))** |
| **Variable-rate information** | If a rate is variable: that it may vary; how it is determined (index and margin); any limitations on increases or decreases. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(i)(A))** |
| **Introductory/discounted rates** | If the initial rate is an introductory rate: the rate that would otherwise apply, or the index and margin if variable. The term "introductory" or "intro" must appear in immediate proximity. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(i)(B))** |
| **Premium initial rates** | If the initial rate is a temporary premium rate higher than the rate that will apply after it expires: the premium rate in at least 16-point type; the rate that will apply afterwards. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(i)(C))** |
| **Penalty rates** | If a rate may increase as a penalty: the increased rate; the triggering events; how long the increased rate will remain in effect. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(i)(D))** |
| **Credit card mandatory APR disclosures** | For credit card accounts: any introductory rate that would apply, and any rate that applies after a premium initial rate expires. These must appear in the table. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(i)(F))** |
| **Fees for issuance or availability** | Annual/periodic fees for having the account; any non-periodic one-time fee for opening the plan; the annualised amount. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(ii))** |
| **Fixed finance charges; minimum interest charge** | Any fixed finance charge; any minimum interest charge if it exceeds $1.00. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(iii))** |
| **Transaction charges** | Any transaction charge for purchases. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(iv))** |
| **Grace period** | The date by which or period within which any credit extended may be repaid without incurring a finance charge. If no grace period: that fact must be disclosed. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(v))** |
| **Balance computation method** | The name of the balance computation method listed in §1026.60(g), or if not listed, an explanation. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(vi))** |
| **Cash advance fee** | Any fee for a cash advance. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(vii))** |
| **Late payment fee** | Any fee for a late payment. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(viii))** |
| **Over-limit fee** | Any fee for exceeding the credit limit. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(ix))** |
| **Balance transfer fee** | Any fee for transferring an outstanding balance. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(x))** |
| **Returned-payment fee** | Any fee for a returned payment. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(xi))** |
| **Required insurance/debt cancellation** | If required as part of the plan: fee and cross-reference to additional information. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(xii))** |
| **Available credit** | If fees for issuance or availability will be charged to the account when opened and those fees are 15% or more of the minimum credit limit: the available credit after those fees are applied. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(xiii))** |
| **Website reference** | For non-charge credit cards: reference to the CFPB website for credit card information. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(xiv))** |
| **Billing error rights reference** | Statement that billing error dispute information is included in the account-opening disclosures. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(xv))** |

**Additional disclosures outside the table (§1026.6(b)(3)–(5)):**
- Circumstances under which charges may be imposed, for all charges that are part of the plan
- Disclosure of rates applicable to each periodic rate and variable-rate information
- Voluntary credit insurance, security interests, and billing rights statement

**Grace period standardised language:** Where a grace period is provided for all features, the phrase "How to Avoid Paying Interest" must be used as the row heading. Where no grace period applies, the phrase "Paying Interest" must be used. **(§1026.6(b)(2)(v))**

## Periodic Statement Requirements — §1026.7

The creditor must furnish a periodic statement for each billing cycle in which a balance remains or a finance charge is imposed. **(§1026.7)**

### Non-Home-Secured Plans (Including Credit Cards) — §1026.7(b)

For credit cards and other non-home-secured revolving plans, each periodic statement must disclose:

| Item | Content required |
|------|-----------------|
| **Previous balance** | Account balance at the beginning of the billing cycle. **(§1026.7(b)(1))** |
| **Transaction identification** | Each credit transaction during the cycle per §1026.8. **(§1026.7(b)(2))** |
| **Credits** | Any credit to the account during the cycle: amount and date. **(§1026.7(b)(3))** |
| **Periodic rates** | Each periodic rate that may compute the finance charge, expressed as an APR; the range of balances to which it applies. **(§1026.7(b)(4))** |
| **Balance subject to interest rate** | The balance to which the periodic rate was applied; how that balance was determined; using the term *Balance Subject to Interest Rate*. **(§1026.7(b)(5))** |
| **Finance charges itemised** | Finance charges attributable to periodic interest rates, grouped under *Interest Charge*, itemised and totalled by type of transaction and labelled *Total Interest*; statement period and calendar-year-to-date totals. Fees grouped separately under *Fees*, with statement period and calendar-year-to-date totals. **(§1026.7(b)(6))** |
| **Annual percentage rate** | The APR(s) applied during the cycle, using the term *Annual Percentage Rate*. **(§1026.7(b)(7))** |
| **Grace period** | Date by which the new balance must be paid to avoid additional finance charges. **(§1026.7(b)(8))** |
| **Address for billing errors** | Address for submitting billing error notices. **(§1026.7(b)(9))** |
| **Closing date and new balance** | Closing date of the billing cycle and account balance on that date. The new balance must be disclosed in the format required by §1026.7(b)(13). **(§1026.7(b)(10))** |
| **Due date and late payment costs** | For credit card accounts: the due date (same day of month each billing cycle); the late payment fee and any increased APR that may result. **(§1026.7(b)(11))** |

#### Minimum Payment Warning — §1026.7(b)(12)

For credit card accounts under non-home-secured plans, the card issuer must provide the following **mandatory repayment disclosures** on each periodic statement:

**(i) In general:**
- The statement: **"Minimum Payment Warning: If you make only the minimum payment each period, you will pay more in interest and it will take you longer to pay off your balance;"**
- The **minimum payment repayment estimate** (calculated per appendix M1) — the time it will take to pay off the current outstanding balance if only minimum payments are made. If less than 2 years, stated in months; otherwise in years rounded to the nearest whole year.
- The **minimum payment total cost estimate** — the total amount paid if only minimum payments are made.
- A statement that the estimates assume only minimum payments are made and no new charges are added.
- A **toll-free telephone number** for credit counselling services.
- The **36-month payoff estimate**: estimated monthly payment to repay the balance in 36 months; a statement that the consumer will repay in 3 years if that payment is made; the total cost estimate for 36-month repayment; and the savings estimate compared to paying only minimums.

**(ii) Negative/no amortisation exception:** If the minimum payment is less than the monthly interest charge (negative or no amortisation), the standard disclosures are replaced by: a warning that making only minimum payments will never pay off the balance and that the balance will increase; the 36-month payoff estimate; and a credit counselling telephone number.

**Format:** Due date, late payment fee, APR, ending balance, minimum payment, and the minimum payment warning must all be grouped together, prominently placed on the first page. The new balance must be clearly visible. Model forms G-18(A) through G-18(H) in appendix G provide compliant formats. **(§1026.7(b)(13))**

#### Deferred Interest Accounts — §1026.7(b)(14)

For accounts with a deferred interest balance, the date by which that balance must be paid in full to avoid retroactive finance charges must appear **on the front of every page** of each periodic statement issued during the deferred interest period, beginning with the first statement that reflects the deferred interest balance. Model Form G-18(H) provides a compliant format.

## Transaction Identification on Periodic Statements — §1026.8

The creditor must identify each credit transaction on the first periodic statement that reflects it:

- **Sale transactions:** Amount, date, and either a brief description of the property or services purchased, or the seller's name and city/state. **(§1026.8(a))**
- **Non-sale transactions (cash advances etc.):** Brief identification of the transaction, amount, and at least one of: the date of the transaction, the date it was debited, or (if the consumer signed a credit document) the date on that document. **(§1026.8(b))**

## Subsequent Disclosure Requirements — §1026.9

### Billing Rights Statement — §1026.9(a)

The creditor must mail or deliver the full billing rights statement required by §1026.6(a)(5) or (b)(5)(iii) at least once per calendar year. As an alternative, the creditor may include a summary billing rights statement substantially similar to Model Form G-4 with each periodic statement.

### Change-in-Terms — §1026.9(c)

**Significant changes — 45-day advance notice required (§1026.9(c)(2)(i)):**

For non-home-secured open-end plans, when a "significant change in account terms" is to be made, the creditor must provide **written notice at least 45 days before the effective date** of the change. Significant changes include:
- Any change to a term required to be disclosed in the account-opening table (§1026.6(b)(1) and (2))
- An increase in the required minimum periodic payment
- A change to a term required to be disclosed under §1026.6(b)(4)
- The acquisition of a security interest

The 45-day requirement does not apply if the consumer has agreed to the particular change (e.g., the consumer requests a credit limit increase requiring new security). **(§1026.9(c)(2)(i)(B))**

**Right to reject — credit card accounts (§1026.9(c)(2)(iv)(B)):**

When a card issuer makes a significant change to the terms of a credit card account, the issuer must generally inform the consumer of the right to reject the change before it becomes effective. If the consumer rejects the change, the consumer's ability to use the account for further advances will be terminated or suspended — but the consumer may pay off the existing balance at the pre-change terms. **(§1026.9(c)(2)(iv)(B)(1)–(3))**

This right to reject does not apply to: increases in the required minimum periodic payment; increases resulting from reevaluation under §1026.52(b)(1)(i); fee adjustments tied to the Consumer Price Index; rate increases on previously reduced fees; or rate increases resulting from the consumer's failure to make the minimum periodic payment within 60 days after the due date.

**Rate increases from failure to make minimum payment — 60-day rule (§1026.9(g)):**

If an APR increase or fee increase results from the consumer's failure to make a minimum periodic payment within 60 days of the due date, the issuer must provide notice that the increase will cease to apply to transactions that occurred before the increase if the consumer makes the required minimum periodic payments on time for six consecutive months following the rate increase. This notice must state the conditions under which the increased rate will be revoked.

## Open-End Advertising — §1026.16

Advertisements for open-end credit products are subject to the following rules:

**Only advertise available terms:** An advertisement may not state terms that the creditor is not actually prepared to offer. **(§1026.16(a))**

**Trigger terms:** If a specific dollar amount of any payment is stated, or if a number of payments is stated, the advertisement must also disclose (with equal prominence and in close proximity): the APR; whether the APR may increase; the amount of any fees for issuance or availability; and the balance to which the rate applies. **(§1026.16(b))**

**"Fixed" rate prohibition:** An advertisement may not describe an APR as "fixed" or use a similar term unless the advertisement also specifies the time period for which the rate will be fixed and states that the rate will not increase during that period. If no such time period is provided, "fixed" may only be used if the rate will not increase while the plan is open. **(§1026.16(f))**

**Promotional/introductory rates (§1026.16(g)):** For non-home-secured plans, if a promotional rate is advertised:
- The term "introductory" or "intro" must appear in immediate proximity to the rate.
- The promotional period end date (when the rate will expire) must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously.
- The APR that will apply after the promotional period must be disclosed.
- The fee that will apply after any promotional fee period must be disclosed.

**Deferred interest offers (§1026.16(h)):** If a deferred interest offer is advertised:
- The deferred interest period must be stated clearly and conspicuously.
- If the phrase "no interest," "no payments," "deferred interest," "same as cash," or similar term is used, the phrase **"if paid in full"** must also appear in immediate proximity in a clear and conspicuous manner.
- The advertisement must disclose that interest will be charged from the date the consumer becomes obligated if the balance is not paid in full within the deferred interest period.

**Balloon payment disclosures (§1026.16(d)(3)):** If an advertisement states a minimum periodic payment and a balloon payment may result if only minimum payments are made, that fact must be disclosed with equal prominence and in close proximity to the minimum payment statement, along with the amount and timing of the balloon.

## Key Points for Agents

- **The Schumer Box is legally mandatory in a specific format.** For credit cards and non-home-secured revolving plans, account-opening disclosures must be tabular, with APR in at least 16-point type, fees in bold text, and content substantially similar to the appendix G-17 model forms. Deviating from the format (e.g., burying fee disclosures in prose) is a §1026.6 violation. **(§1026.6(b)(1))**
- **Minimum payment warning is mandatory, not discretionary, and uses prescribed language.** The exact statutory warning ("Minimum Payment Warning: If you make only the minimum payment…") must appear on every credit card periodic statement, with the estimated repayment time and total cost. The 36-month payoff comparison is also mandatory. Omitting or paraphrasing this warning is a §1026.7(b)(12) violation. **(§1026.7(b)(12))**
- **Deferred interest payoff date must appear on the front of every statement.** For any account carrying a deferred interest balance, the payoff date must be on the front of every page of every periodic statement during the deferred interest period. This is not satisfied by a disclosure in the middle of the statement or in fine print. **(§1026.7(b)(14))**
- **45 days' notice is required before raising rates or fees on credit cards — not 30.** Significant changes to credit card terms require 45 days' advance written notice. The consumer must be informed of the right to reject the change and close the account at existing terms. **(§1026.9(c)(2))**
- **"Introductory" must appear next to every promotional rate.** In advertisements and account-opening disclosures, any rate that is an introductory rate must be labelled as such — in immediate proximity to the rate itself, not just in a footnote or at the end of the document. **(§1026.16(g)(3))**
- **"If paid in full" must accompany any deferred interest advertisement.** Any advertisement using the phrases "no interest," "same as cash," "no payments," or similar must include "if paid in full" in clear and conspicuous proximity. Omitting this pairing is a §1026.16(h) violation and a likely UDAAP risk. **(§1026.16(h)(3))**
- **Highest-APR allocation applies above the minimum.** For credit cards, any payment above the required minimum must be applied to the balance with the highest APR first. This is a structural requirement — allocating excess payments to low-APR balances to maximise interest charges is prohibited. **(§1026.53; Subpart G)**
- **Penalty rate reversal at 6 months.** If a penalty APR is applied for a 60-day delinquency, and the consumer then makes 6 consecutive on-time minimum payments, the issuer must remove the penalty rate from pre-increase balances. This must be disclosed when the penalty rate is imposed. **(§1026.9(g); §1026.55)**
