# Regulation E — Prepaid Accounts and Gift Cards (§§1005.18–1005.20)

## What Is a Prepaid Account?

A **prepaid account** is a type of "account" for Reg E purposes. **(§1005.2(b)(3))** It includes:

- **Payroll card accounts:** Cards loaded with wage or salary payments by an employer.
- **General-purpose reloadable (GPR) prepaid cards:** Cards marketed to the general public, loadable with funds by or on behalf of the consumer, and usable at multiple unaffiliated merchants or ATMs.
- **Other prepaid accounts:** Accounts loaded with funds at the consumer's direction, or where the consumer's principal means of access is a prepaid card (not a credit card, debit card linked to a checking account, or check).

Government benefit accounts (EBT) are treated as accounts under §1005.15, not §1005.18, although §1005.18 cross-references government benefit accounts for some short-form disclosure requirements.

## Pre-Acquisition Disclosures — Two-Tier Structure (§1005.18(b))

Before a consumer acquires a prepaid account, the issuer must provide **both** a short form and a long form disclosure simultaneously.

### Short Form Disclosure

The short form is a tabular "fee box" — analogous to the Schumer Box for credit cards — designed for comparison at point of sale.

**Mandatory items in the short form (§1005.18(b)(2)):**

1. **Periodic fee** — the fee charged for holding the account (e.g., monthly maintenance fee)
2. **Per-purchase fee** — the fee charged each time the card is used for a purchase
3. **ATM withdrawal fee (in-network)** — fee for cash withdrawals at in-network ATMs
4. **ATM withdrawal fee (out-of-network)** — fee for cash withdrawals at out-of-network ATMs
5. **Cash reload fee** — fee for loading funds onto the card
6. **ATM balance inquiry fee (in-network)**
7. **ATM balance inquiry fee (out-of-network)**
8. **Customer service fee** — per-call or per-month fee for live or automated customer service
9. **Inactivity fee** — fee charged after a period of account inactivity
10. **Count of additional fee types** — the total number of additional fee types that may be imposed (other than those disclosed in items 1–9)

**Additional fee types:** If the issuer charges other fees beyond items 1–9, the short form must disclose the **two highest-revenue additional fee types** for the prepaid account program (based on revenue generated in the prior 24 months, or reasonably estimated if the program is new). The selection must be reassessed at least every **24 months**. **(§1005.18(b)(2)(ix))**

**Type size requirements:**
- Fees disclosed under items 1–9 and any additional fee types must be in **bold 15-point type** or larger.
- The heading "Prepaid Account Fees" or a substantially similar heading must appear in **bold 8-point type** or larger.
- All other text in the short form must be in **8-point type** or larger.

**FDIC/NCUA insurance status:** The short form must state whether funds in the prepaid account are FDIC/NCUA insured, using one of five prescribed disclosure variants:

| Variant | When to use |
|---------|------------|
| A | Account eligible for FDIC/NCUA pass-through insurance; no identity verification required |
| B | Account eligible for FDIC/NCUA pass-through insurance; identity registration/verification required |
| C | Account not eligible for FDIC/NCUA pass-through insurance |
| D | Issuer not FDIC/NCUA insured — funds not insured |
| E | Insured institution but funds not eligible for pass-through coverage |

**CFPB website reference:** The short form must include a reference directing the consumer to the CFPB website for general prepaid account information.

**Payroll card statement:** If the prepaid account is a payroll card, the short form must state that the consumer is **not required to accept the payroll card** as a condition of employment or receipt of wages. **(§1005.18(b)(2)(xii))**

**Overdraft credit feature:** If the account offers an overdraft credit feature (hybrid prepaid-credit card), the short form must include the Reg Z disclosures required by 12 CFR 1026.60(e)(1) for the credit feature.

### Long Form Disclosure

The long form must disclose **all fees** that may be imposed in connection with the prepaid account, and all terms and conditions. **(§1005.18(b)(4))**

- Format: any clear and conspicuous format
- Must be provided simultaneously with the short form before acquisition
- May be provided in electronic form to the extent permitted under the E-Sign Act
- Must be available on the issuer's website and by telephone (audio or text format)

**Payroll card alternative:** A payroll card account short form and long form may be provided to the consumer within a reasonable time before the first use of the card, rather than pre-acquisition, where the employer distributes payroll cards and the consumer does not have a meaningful opportunity to review them before the first payroll is loaded. **(§1005.18(h)(2))**

## Account Access and Periodic Statements (§1005.18(c))

Because many prepaid accounts do not generate traditional periodic statements:

**Option 1 — Electronic account history:** The financial institution must make available to the consumer **12 months of electronic transaction history** at all times through a website, mobile application, or other electronic means. **(§1005.18(c)(1)(ii))**

**Option 2 — Written history on request:** The financial institution must provide **24 months of written transaction history** upon the consumer's request, within a reasonable time. **(§1005.18(c)(1)(iii))**

**Option 3 — Periodic statement:** The financial institution may provide periodic statements in lieu of the above.

**Fee summary:** The statement or electronic history must include a summary of fees assessed for the prior calendar month and the prior calendar year to date. **(§1005.18(c)(5))**

---

## Disclosure of Fees and Change-in-Terms (§1005.18(f))

**Initial disclosures:** The long form pre-acquisition disclosure serves as the initial disclosure for §1005.7 purposes. **(§1005.18(f)(1))**

**Change-in-terms notice:** The standard 21-day advance notice requirement in §1005.8(a) applies to changes in terms covered by the pre-acquisition disclosures. However:
- If the issuer discloses third-party fees in the long form, a change-in-terms notice is not required solely to reflect a change in that third-party fee amount. **(§1005.18(f)(2))**
- If the issuer provides Reg Z disclosures for an overdraft credit feature in the long form, a change-in-terms notice is not required solely to reflect a change in fees or terms of that credit feature.

**Access device disclosures:** The name of the financial institution, website URL, and telephone number must appear on the prepaid account access device (card). If no physical card is provided, the disclosure must appear on the website, mobile application, or other entry point. **(§1005.18(f)(3))**

## Hybrid Prepaid-Credit Cards (§1005.18(g))

A "hybrid prepaid-credit card" is a prepaid card that can also access a covered separate credit feature as defined under 12 CFR 1026.61.

**Non-discrimination requirement:** A financial institution must provide the same account terms, conditions, and features to any prepaid account that does not have a covered separate credit feature as it provides to prepaid accounts with such a feature in the same prepaid account program. An exception permits charging higher fees on prepaid accounts with a covered credit feature, if the fee difference reflects the incremental cost of the credit feature. **(§1005.18(g))**

---

## Liability and Error Resolution for Unverified Prepaid Accounts (§1005.18(e))

For prepaid accounts that are **not payroll cards** and **not government benefit accounts**, a financial institution that has not yet completed consumer identification and verification (CIP) is **not required** to comply with the standard §1005.6 liability caps or §1005.11 error resolution timelines, provided it:

1. Has disclosed to the consumer the risks of not registering the account, using a notice substantially similar to Model Form A-7.
2. Has concluded its CIP process but could not verify the consumer's identity (and disclosed the risk).
3. Does not have a CIP process, but made the alternative disclosure required by §1005.18(d)(1)(ii).

**Once verification is complete:** The financial institution must apply full §1005.6 liability limits and §1005.11 error resolution timelines for all transfers that occur after verification. **(§1005.18(e)(3)(iii))**

## Internet Posting of Prepaid Account Agreements (§1005.19)

**Submission obligation:** Issuers with **3,000 or more open prepaid accounts** must submit prepaid account agreements to the CFPB on a rolling basis. Submissions are due within **30 days** after an issuer offers, amends, or ceases to offer any agreement. **(§1005.19(b)(1))**

Each submission must include:
- Identifying information about the issuer (name, address, RSSD ID or tax ID number)
- Effective date of the agreement
- Name of the program manager, if any
- Names of other relevant parties (e.g., employer for payroll cards, government agency for benefit programs)
- The full text of the agreement, including all fee information

**Amended agreements:** Amended agreements must be submitted within 30 days after the change becomes effective. **(§1005.19(b)(2))**

**Withdrawal:** If an issuer ceases to offer a prepaid account agreement, it must notify the CFPB within 30 days. **(§1005.19(b)(3))**

**Public posting:** Agreements submitted to the Bureau (except those under the de minimis or product testing exceptions) must be posted on the issuer's publicly available website in a prominent and readily accessible location, without requiring submission of personal information. **(§1005.19(c))**

**Individual consumer access:** For any open account, the issuer must either:
- Post the consumer's specific agreement on its website (accessible only to that consumer); or
- Provide the consumer with a copy of the agreement within **5 business days** of request. Agreements provided by request must be in paper form unless the consumer agrees to electronic delivery. **(§1005.19(d))**

**Exceptions:**
- **De minimis:** Fewer than 3,000 open prepaid accounts — exempt from submission and posting. **(§1005.19(b)(4)(i))**
- **Product testing:** Agreement used for a limited consumer group for a limited period, with fewer than 3,000 open accounts — exempt until thresholds are crossed. **(§1005.19(b)(5))**
- **Initial submission date:** First submissions were due no later than May 1, 2019 for agreements offered as of April 1, 2019. **(§1005.19(f))**

## Gift Cards and Gift Certificates (§1005.20)

### Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Gift certificate** | Prepaid for personal/family/household use; redeemable at a single merchant or affiliated group; not reloadable or reloadable only in limited circumstances |
| **Store gift card** | Prepaid; redeemable at a single merchant or affiliated group; may be reloadable |
| **General-use prepaid card** | Prepaid; redeemable at multiple **unaffiliated** merchants or ATMs |
| **Loyalty/award/promotional gift card** | Issued in connection with a loyalty, award, or promotional program; exempt from most §1005.20 requirements if it meets the prescribed on-card disclosure criteria |

### Exclusions from §1005.20

The following are **not** "gift certificates," "store gift cards," or "general-use prepaid cards" and are **not** subject to §1005.20: **(§1005.20(b))**
- Cards usable solely for telephone services
- Reloadable cards not marketed or labelled as gift cards
- Loyalty, award, or promotional gift cards (subject to their own lighter disclosure regime)
- Cards not marketed to the general public
- Cards issued in paper form only
- Cards redeemable solely for admission to events at a particular location group

### Dormancy, Inactivity, and Service Fee Rules (§1005.20(d))

A dormancy, inactivity, or service fee may **not** be imposed unless **all three** conditions are met:

1. There has been **no activity** with respect to the certificate or card in the **1-year period** ending on the date on which the fee is imposed.
2. The following information is **clearly and conspicuously** disclosed **on the certificate or card**:
   - The amount of the fee (or how it will be determined)
   - How often the fee may be assessed
   - That such fee may be assessed for inactivity
3. **No more than one** dormancy, inactivity, or service fee is imposed in any given **calendar month**.

### Expiration of Funds (§1005.20(e))

No person may sell or issue a gift certificate, store gift card, or general-use prepaid card with an expiration date unless:

1. The issuer has established policies and procedures giving consumers a reasonable opportunity to purchase a replacement card with **at least 5 years remaining** until the certificate or card expiration date.
2. The **expiration date for the underlying funds** is at least the **later of:**
   - **5 years** from initial issuance (for gift certificates and general-use prepaid cards) or the date on which funds were last loaded (for store gift cards); or
   - The certificate or card expiration date.
3. The following are disclosed on the certificate or card:
   - The expiration date for the underlying funds (or that the underlying funds do not expire)
   - A toll-free telephone number and, if maintained, a website for obtaining a replacement after card expiry
   - If the card has an expiration date shorter than the fund expiration date: a statement that the card expires but funds do not, and that the consumer can contact the issuer for a replacement
4. **No fee may be imposed** for replacing the certificate or card or for providing the remaining balance prior to fund expiry.

### Additional Fee Disclosures on the Card (§1005.20(f))

For any fee type other than dormancy/inactivity/service fees, the following must be provided on or with the card:
- The type of fee
- The amount (or how it will be determined)
- The conditions under which the fee may be imposed

A toll-free telephone number and, if maintained, a website for obtaining fee information must appear on the certificate or card.

## Key Points for Agents

- **Payroll cards are fully regulated.** A payroll card account is a prepaid account under §1005.2(b)(3). The employer cannot require acceptance as a condition of employment. Full Reg E applies: two-tier pre-acquisition disclosure, liability limits, error resolution. **(§§1005.2(b)(3); 1005.18(b)(2)(xii))**
- **Pre-acquisition disclosure timing is strict.** The short form and long form must both be available before the consumer acquires the account. For online sign-ups, this means before the consumer commits to the account. **(§1005.18(b)(1))**
- **The short form fee box has prescribed type sizes.** Fees must appear in bold 15-point type. Failing to meet the type size requirement is a technical violation even if the dollar amounts are correct. **(§1005.18(b)(2))**
- **Unverified prepaid accounts have reduced consumer protections — but the issuer must disclose this.** If an issuer does not verify the consumer's identity, it is exempt from liability limits and error resolution, but only if it has disclosed the risk to the consumer using language substantially similar to the model form. Failure to disclose the risk makes the exemption unavailable. **(§1005.18(e)(3))**
- **Agreement posting is mandatory at 3,000 accounts.** Once a prepaid program reaches 3,000 open accounts, the issuer must submit agreements to the CFPB and post them publicly. The de minimis threshold applies to the number of open accounts, not active ones. **(§1005.19(b)(4))**
- **Gift card dormancy fees require 12 months of inactivity.** One fee per calendar month maximum. The trigger, amount, and frequency must be disclosed on the card itself — not in accompanying materials, on a website, or via a QR code. **(§1005.20(d))**
- **Gift card fund expiry before 5 years is prohibited.** The 5-year minimum applies from initial issuance (gift certificates) or last load (reloadable store gift cards). Card expiry before fund expiry is permitted only if replacement is provided at no charge and specific disclosures appear on the card. **(§1005.20(e))**
